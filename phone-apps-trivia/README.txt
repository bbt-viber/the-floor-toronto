PHONE APPS TRIVIA — 40 images (15 Easy / 15 Medium / 10 Hard)
All images 1200x1000 PNG. Filename = the answer.

Each card is the app's icon, shown the way it looks on a phone (rounded
corners), with NO text naming the app anywhere on it. Icons are the
current official App Store artwork (November 2026), with the exceptions
noted below. Single-letter marks are kept where the letter IS the logo
(Facebook's "f", Netflix's "N", LinkedIn's "in", the "S" on Shopify's
bag) — same rule as earlier sets.

EASY (15)
Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, TikTok, Spotify, Netflix,
Google Maps, Gmail, Snapchat, Messenger, Roblox, Pinterest, Reddit,
Discord

MEDIUM (15)
X, Telegram, Zoom, Duolingo, Airbnb, Twitch, LinkedIn, Dropbox, Slack,
Shopify, Temple Run, SoundCloud, Google Drive, ChatGPT,
Plants vs. Zombies

HARD (10)
Signal, Notion, Yelp, OpenTable, DoorDash, Canadian Tire, VSCO, CapCut,
Threads, Bluesky

NOTES / JUDGMENT CALLS
- Zoom: the current store icon is literally the word "zoom", which
  breaks the no-name rule, so this card uses Zoom's classic camera mark
  instead — the white video camera on Zoom blue that the app used for
  years.
- Yelp: the store icon has "yelp" written under the starburst. The word
  was removed; the burst (Yelp's actual symbol) is kept.
- Snapchat: the store is currently serving a seasonal variant — the
  usual ghost on a spiderweb background. The ghost itself is unchanged.
- Roblox: the current store icon is the white tilted tile on blue (the
  older grey tile has been retired).
- Google Maps uses the current rainbow-pin artwork, not the old
  pin-over-a-map icon.
- Canadian Tire sits in Hard for a general audience; for a Canadian
  group it will likely play much easier.
