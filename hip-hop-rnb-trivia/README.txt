HIP HOP & R&B SONGS TRIVIA — EASIER VERSION — 40 images
(15 Easy / 15 Medium / 10 Hard). All images 1200x1000 PNG.
Filename = the answer.

This is the easier remake of the Hip Hop & R&B set (Nov 2026): Easy and
Medium are now built from songs the general public knows — crossover
hits, wedding and party staples — while Hard stays aimed at fans. The
original, harder set is untouched in hip-hop-rnb-trivia.zip.

HOW IT PLAYS: each card shows a song title and the year it was
released. Players name the ARTIST. The artist's name appears nowhere
on the card — it's the filename.

RULES USED: one song per artist, so all 40 answers are different.
Where a song has a featured guest, the answer is the lead artist
(Yeah! = Usher, Crazy in Love = Beyoncé, Empire State of Mind = Jay-Z,
See You Again = Wiz Khalifa, Sunflower = Post Malone). Thrift Shop's
answer is the act name, Macklemore & Ryan Lewis. Years are the year
the song was released as a single.

WHAT CHANGED VS THE ORIGINAL SET
- Easy: Hey Ya! replaces Ms. Jackson (Outkast either way); the other
  14 are unchanged.
- Medium: rebuilt around mainstream hits. In: Lose Yourself, Work It,
  Low, Thrift Shop, All of Me, I Like It, Sunflower, Old Town Road.
  Out: Still D.R.E., Stan, Get Ur Freak On, Crank That, Redbone,
  Bodak Yellow, Sicko Mode. (Cutting Crank That also removes the old
  card whose title gave away the artist's name.)
- Hard: Kill Bill moves down from Medium; Two Weeks (FKA twigs) is
  out. Artists dropped from the set entirely: Dr. Dre, Childish
  Gambino, Travis Scott, Soulja Boy, FKA twigs.

NOTES ON YEARS
- Old Town Road first appeared as an independent release in December
  2018; the card uses 2019, its commercial single release and chart
  year.
- Panda first appeared independently in December 2015; the card uses
  its 2016 commercial single release.
- Kill Bill is on a 2022 album but was released as a single in 2023.
- Anxiety circulated as a 2019 demo before its official 2025 release.
