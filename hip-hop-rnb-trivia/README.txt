HIP HOP & R&B SONGS TRIVIA — 40 images (15 Easy / 15 Medium / 10 Hard)
All images 1200x1000 PNG. Filename = the answer.

HOW IT PLAYS: each card shows a song title and the year it was released.
Players name the ARTIST. The artist's name appears nowhere on the card —
it's the filename.

RULES USED: one song per artist, so all 40 answers are different. Where a
song has a featured guest, the answer is the lead artist (Yeah! = Usher,
Crazy in Love = Beyoncé, Empire State of Mind = Jay-Z, See You Again =
Wiz Khalifa). Years are the year the song was released as a single.

NOTES / JUDGMENT CALLS
- Crank That (Soulja Boy): the artist's name is part of the song's actual
  title, so this Medium card is effectively a freebie. Swap it out if you
  want the tier to stay honest.
- Four years have a footnote: Still D.R.E. is sometimes listed as 2000;
  Panda first appeared independently in December 2015 before its 2016
  commercial single release; Kill Bill is on a 2022 album but was released
  as a single in 2023; Anxiety circulated as a 2019 demo before its
  official release in 2025. The cards use the single-release year.
