JUDGMENT CALLS / NOTES
----------------------
- Central Perk: the wide set photo had the "Central Perk" logo painted on
  the window. That logo was removed digitally and the window glass filled
  in; the orange couch and the rest of the set carry the clue.
- Jack and Judy's House barely appears on screen, so its image is the
  1980s flashback to the Geller kitchen (young Chandler, Fat Monica and
  afro-haired Ross) from "The One Where Ross Got High".
- The Montauk Beach House exterior was never an iconic shot, so its image
  is Joey buried as a sand mermaid inside the sand-filled beach house.
- The Geller Cup: no episode still actually shows the trophy, and replica
  photos were inaccurate, so the image is the original prop photographed
  on display at FriendsFest (beside Ross's "Science Boy" comic). The small
  winner plates on the trophy are part of the prop itself.
- Phoebe Buffay's image is her busking with her guitar; the blurred poster
  fragments behind her are subway set dressing.
- Hugsy the Penguin and The Yellow Door Frame are photographed as the
  props themselves (Hugsy in his goggles and nightcap; the frame around
  the peephole of the purple door).
- Regina Phalange has no costume of her own; her image is Phoebe on the
  phone in 'The One With Ross's Wedding' storyline, where she calls London
  pretending to be Ross's brain doctor, Dr. Regina Phalange - the alias's
  first and most famous appearance.
- Dr. Drake Ramoray's image is a still of Joey in character on the soap
  set, not a posed portrait.
- The Days of Our Lives Set deliberately uses a production photo of a
  soap being filmed (cameras, boom mic, lighting rig and crew around an
  actor) so the card reads as the TV set itself and cannot be confused
  with the Drake Ramoray character card.
- Arrows: red arrows were added to three images to make the intended
  subject unambiguous - Mike Hannigan, Mrs. Whiskerson (the cat), and
  Pat the Dog (the statue).
