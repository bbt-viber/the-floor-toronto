NOTES / JUDGMENT CALLS
----------------------
- Small generic descriptor text was kept where it isn't the brand name
  (e.g. "PEANUT CARAMEL BAR" on PayDay, "HARD CANDY" on Jolly Rancher,
  "Buttery Toffee Crunch..." on Skor, flavour lists on several bags).
- Single-letter marks printed on the candies themselves were kept, same
  rule as the NBA monograms: the "m" on M&M's, the "S" on Skittles,
  the "M" crown marks on Maynards Wine Gums.
- Kit Kat: the whole white oval behind the name was removed, not just
  the letters — the red wrapper and wafer fingers carry it.
- Aero: a tiny "®" symbol remains floating on the wrapper.
- Milky Way: a small "TM" remains beside the cloud graphic.
- Mars (revised): the name is removed but the signature red swoosh
  stripe under it was deliberately kept as a clue.
- Oh Henry! (revised): new photo — the wrapper plus the bar cut in
  half, showing the peanuts, caramel and fudge layers; the wrapper's
  "PEANUTTY • CARAMEL • FUDGE" descriptor line is kept.
- Twix (revised): new photo — two fingers sliding out of the gold
  pack with the biscuit and caramel layers visible in the cut ends.
- Skor (revised): the name is removed but the small gold crown from
  the wordmark was deliberately kept as a branding clue.
- Starburst: the fill where the name sat is a smooth yellow splash and
  the two wrapped chews lost their tiny wrapper print.
- Swedish Fish: a tiny partial lettering fragment remains on one small
  fish inside the bag's zip window — too small/cut off to read.
- Bounty: the edit extended the tropical palm/coconut scene across the
  whole wrapper (more redesign than the others).
- PayDay: the centre of the wrapper filled in solid blue.
- Haribo Goldbears: the bottom banner slogan now ends mid-sentence
  ("THE HAPPY WORLD OF") where the brand word was removed.
- 100 Grand: a pile of fun-size minis — the name was removed from every
  wrapper; the descriptor lines remain.
- Cherry Blossom: retail display box — the name was removed from all
  12 small boxes plus the display front/side.
