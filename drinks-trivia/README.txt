DRINKS TRIVIA — 40 images (15 Easy / 15 Medium / 10 Hard)
All images 1200x1000 PNG. Filename = the answer.

Branded drinks are shown as the real product with the brand wordmark
removed; the packaging shape, colours, logos and descriptors that act as
clues are kept. Cocktails are shown with their signature garnish or
ingredients visible, per the brief.
